The books

Two novels. One long road home.

Each book stands on its own. Both follow someone who was taught to stay quiet, and the slow, stubborn work of finally being whole.

Newest novel

Trials of Patience

Her mind locked the worst of it away. Healing means opening the door.

Patience Kelly grew up loved, in a devout Christian family in small-town Indiana, carrying a secret she didn't know she was keeping. College was supposed to be her fresh start. Instead came a controlling boyfriend, an assault, and a pastor who blamed her for it.

A story about trauma and survival, faith lost and found in unexpected places, and what it means to be truly seen and loved.

Award-winning debut

Nephi's Courage

Story of a Bad Mormon

He loves God. He keeps the commandments. And he's gay.

Nephi Willard has done everything a faithful Latter-day Saint is supposed to do in Utah's Happy Valley. He served a mission. He held his callings. Then he tells his bishop the truth, and the life he built starts to come apart.

When he meets someone who shows him a future he never let himself imagine, Nephi has to decide whether he can keep his faith and still choose love.

About the author

Hi, I'm Rory.

I write about people who were taught to hide parts of themselves, and what happens when they stop.

I was born and raised in Utah and grew up in the Mormon church. Later I spent years in Seattle, hiking every trail I could find. For a long time I carried stories around in my head. Eventually I started writing them down.

My characters wrestle with faith, family, sexuality, and trauma, and they don't always get tidy answers. What they get is a chance to be seen as they really are. I think everyone deserves that.

I'm an independent author, which means there's no publishing house behind these books. Just me, a few brave early readers, and you. Thank you for being here.
